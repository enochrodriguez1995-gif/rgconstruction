More Than a Business — A Family Legacy.

For Gonzalo Rodriguez, RG Construction is more than a company — it's the result of years of late nights, early mornings, and faith in God's guidance. With a vision and unwavering belief in honest work, he built a legacy that builds more than houses; it builds trust.

What started with a single truck, a set of tools, and a prayer has grown into one of Louisiana's most trusted construction companies. But behind every fence installed, every wall remodeled, every floor laid — there is a family that made it possible.

Gonzalo's wife and children have stood by his side through every challenge. They are the heart of RG Construction and the reason this business doesn't just deliver craftsmanship — it delivers care with faith and integrity.

We treat every home like it's our own family's home. That's not a slogan — that's our promise.

Fence Installation & Repair

From privacy fences to decorative installations, we create durable, beautiful fencing solutions that enhance your property's curb appeal and security. We work with wood, vinyl, chain link, aluminum, and ornamental materials.

What's Included:

  • Free consultation & property assessment
  • Materials sourcing & quality selection
  • Professional installation with proper spacing
  • Gate installation & hardware
  • Cleanup & site restoration

Our Process:

1

Design Consultation - We discuss your vision, property layout, and material preferences

2

Site Preparation - We mark property lines and prepare the ground

3

Professional Installation - Expert teams install with precision and care

4

Final Inspection - We ensure everything meets our quality standards
